Chapter 1 · Wireless interference

Two antennas draw a family of hyperbolas

Send one signal from two antennas and the waves add up in some places and cancel in others. Every point where they add, or cancel, has the same difference in distance to the two antennas. A curve of constant distance difference is a hyperbola.

Chapter 2 · Spacetime

The same curve, with a different meaning

Spacetime diagrams are full of hyperbolas too. There, every point on one is the same time away on a clock that travels from the origin. Compare the two kinds side by side, then squeeze all of spacetime into a diamond.

Chapter 3 · Hyperbolic geometry

From one hyperbola to the Poincaré disk

Project the spacetime hyperbola onto a line and the whole infinite curve fits inside a short interval. Do the same in one more dimension and you get the Poincaré disk, which has so much room near its rim that a branching tree fits with every edge the same length.

Chapter 4 · Plane coloring

How many colors does the flat plane need?

Color every point of the plane so that no two points exactly 1 apart share a color. A pattern of hexagons does it with 7. The distances behind that pattern come from a simple walk: one step, a 60° turn, another step.
