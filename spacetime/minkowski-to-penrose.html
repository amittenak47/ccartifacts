Minkowski to Penrose

Watch a Minkowski spacetime diagram squeeze into a Penrose diamond.

Minkowski spacetime diagram with hyperbolas of constant interval and a grid of constant time and position, morphing into a Penrose diamond as a slider moves or the play button runs.

The light-cone X stays straight at 45° by design and shrinks so its ends become the midpoints of the diamond's edges. The hyperbolas curl inward and bunch up along the edges ℐ⁺ and ℐ⁻. Lines of constant t pinch to the side corners i⁰; lines of constant x pinch to the top and bottom corners i⁺ and i⁻.
