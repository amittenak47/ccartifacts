Two Steps, One Turn

The √3·d walk on a triangular lattice: one step, a 60° turn, another step.

The 60° is the exterior angle, how far you turn off your heading. The 120° is the interior angle the two steps enclose. Dropping a perpendicular splits the triangle into two 30-60-90 halves, each with base √3/2·d.
